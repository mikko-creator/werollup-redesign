Shop Cannabis In-Store or Order Online for Pickup & Delivery

As the first legal, licensed cannabis dispensary in San Pedro, We Roll Up continues to pioneer change for the better — and that includes bringing the dispensary experience directly to your door with our convenient, discreet, and reliable delivery service. We believe access should never be a barrier, which is why we’ve made it easier than ever to get exactly what you need, whether you prefer to browse our welcoming in-store environment or place a delivery order from the comfort of home. Our doors are open and our delivery runs seven days a week, because your schedule matters and your comfort matters even more. We serve anyone and everyone from all walks of life — recreational novices trying cannabis for the first time, devoted enthusiasts who know their preferences inside and out, medicinal patients counting on consistency and care, locals who’ve made us part of their weekly routine, and visitors discovering everything San Pedro has to offer. If you’re 21 or older with a valid ID, getting what you need from We Roll Up has never been simpler — shop in store or place your delivery order and let us handle the rest. Our knowledgeable, passionate, and genuinely helpful staff members are committed to making every interaction exceptional, whether they’re guiding you through options at the counter or delivering your order straight to your doorstep with the efficiency, discretion, and care you deserve. However you choose to shop with us, the We Roll Up experience comes to you.
